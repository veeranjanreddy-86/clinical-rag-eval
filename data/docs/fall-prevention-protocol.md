# Inpatient Fall Prevention Protocol

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional organization ("Maple Ridge Health System").
> Not clinical guidance. Contains no real patient data.

## Risk Assessment

Every adult inpatient receives a fall risk screening on admission, at every shift change, after a
fall, and after any transfer between units. A screening score of 45 or higher on the unit's fall
risk scale identifies a patient as high fall risk.

## Universal Precautions

For all patients: keep the bed in the lowest position with wheels locked, keep the call light and
personal items within reach, provide non-slip footwear, and keep the room free of clutter.

## High Fall Risk Interventions

Patients at high fall risk additionally receive a yellow wristband, a bed or chair exit alarm,
scheduled toileting rounds every 2 hours while awake, and a sign on the door. Hourly purposeful
rounding is documented in the nursing record.

## After a Fall

After any fall, the nurse assesses the patient for injury before moving them, notifies the provider
within 15 minutes, and completes a post-fall huddle with the team within the same shift. A safety
event report is filed within 24 hours. Patients on anticoagulants who fall receive a provider
evaluation for possible head injury.

## Family Engagement

Family members are invited to help by reminding the patient to call for assistance before getting
up, and by notifying staff when they leave the bedside.
