# Prior Authorization Policy: Advanced Imaging

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional health plan ("Maple Ridge Health Plan").
> Not a real payer policy. Contains no real patient data.

## Scope

This policy applies to outpatient advanced imaging: MRI, CT, PET, and nuclear cardiology studies.
Plain radiographs (X-ray) and ultrasound do not require prior authorization.

## Submission Requirements

Requests are submitted through the provider portal by the ordering clinician's office. Each request
must include the CPT code, the ICD-10 diagnosis code, a summary of conservative treatment already
tried, and the date of the most recent clinical examination. Incomplete requests are pended for
up to 5 business days while the office is asked for missing information.

## Review Timeframes

Standard requests receive a decision within 3 business days of receiving a complete request.
Urgent requests, where a delay could seriously jeopardize the patient's health, receive a decision
within 72 hours. Retrospective requests submitted after the service are accepted only within
14 calendar days of the date of service.

## Clinical Criteria

Lumbar spine MRI for low back pain is approved when symptoms persist after 6 weeks of conservative
therapy, or sooner when red-flag findings are documented, such as progressive neurological deficit.
Repeat imaging of the same body part within 12 months requires documentation of a change in
clinical status.

## Approval Validity

An approved imaging authorization is valid for 60 calendar days from the approval date. If the
study is not completed within that window, a new request must be submitted.

## Denials and Peer-to-Peer Review

When a request is denied, the ordering clinician may request a peer-to-peer conversation with a
plan medical director within 5 business days of the denial notice. A formal appeal may be filed
within 60 calendar days.
