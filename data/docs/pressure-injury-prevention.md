# Pressure Injury Prevention Guideline

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional organization ("Maple Ridge Health System").
> Not clinical guidance. Contains no real patient data.

## Skin Assessment

A head-to-toe skin assessment is completed within 8 hours of admission and once every shift
thereafter. Particular attention is given to bony prominences such as the sacrum, heels, and hips,
and to skin under medical devices.

## Risk Scoring

Pressure injury risk is scored on admission and daily using the unit's standardized risk scale.
A score of 18 or lower indicates the patient is at risk and the prevention bundle is started.

## Prevention Bundle

The prevention bundle includes repositioning at least every 2 hours for patients in bed, a pressure
redistribution mattress, heel offloading with pillows or boots, moisture management, and a
nutrition consult when intake is poor.

## Wound Care Nurse Consult

A consult to the wound care nurse is placed for any pressure injury of stage 2 or higher, any
unstageable injury, or any suspected deep tissue injury. The consult should be placed within
24 hours of identification.

## Documentation

New pressure injuries are photographed according to the photography policy, measured, and staged.
Hospital-acquired pressure injuries of stage 3 or higher are reported as safety events.
