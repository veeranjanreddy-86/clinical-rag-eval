# Skilled Nursing Facility Transfer FAQ

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional organization ("Maple Ridge Health System").
> Not a real coverage policy. Contains no real patient data.

## Who needs a skilled nursing facility?

A skilled nursing facility (SNF) is considered when a patient needs daily skilled nursing or
rehabilitation therapy that cannot be safely provided at home, but no longer needs hospital-level care.

## Is there a minimum hospital stay?

Under the fictional plan rules used in this FAQ, SNF coverage requires a qualifying inpatient stay of
at least 3 consecutive midnights. Time spent in observation status does not count toward the 3 midnights.

## How are facilities selected?

The case manager gives the patient a list of facilities that have accepted the referral and that are
in-network for the patient's plan. The patient or their representative selects the facility. The
case manager documents the choice and any facilities that declined.

## What information goes to the facility?

The transfer packet includes the discharge summary, the reconciled medication list, the most recent
therapy evaluation, code status, and isolation precautions if any. A nurse-to-nurse verbal handoff
is completed by phone before the patient leaves.

## How long does insurer review take?

For members of the fictional plan, SNF admission requests receive a decision within 1 business day.
If authorization is still pending at the time of medical readiness, the case manager escalates to
the utilization review nurse.
