# Home Health Referral Policy

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional organization ("Maple Ridge Health System").
> Not a real coverage policy. Contains no real patient data.

## Eligibility for Home Health Services

A patient qualifies for skilled home health services when they are considered homebound, need
intermittent skilled nursing or therapy, and are under the care of a physician who certifies the
plan of care. Homebound means leaving home requires considerable and taxing effort, such as the
help of another person or an assistive device.

## Face-to-Face Encounter

A face-to-face encounter related to the primary reason for home health must occur within 90 days
before or 30 days after the start of care. The encounter is documented by the certifying physician
or an allowed non-physician practitioner.

## Making the Referral

The case manager sends the referral packet to the selected agency, including the discharge summary,
the reconciled medication list, wound care orders if applicable, and therapy orders. Patients are
offered a choice of at least two participating agencies, and the patient's choice is documented.

## Start of Care

The home health agency completes the start-of-care visit within 48 hours of discharge unless the
physician orders a different start date.

## Plan of Care Updates

The certifying physician reviews and signs the home health plan of care every 60 days while
services continue.
