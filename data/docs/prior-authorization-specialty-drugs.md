# Prior Authorization Policy: Specialty Medications

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional health plan ("Maple Ridge Health Plan").
> Not a real payer policy. Drug classes are generic; no real formulary is described.

## Scope

This policy covers self-administered and clinician-administered specialty medications listed on the
fictional plan's specialty tier, including biologic therapies for inflammatory conditions.

## Step Therapy

Before a biologic therapy is approved for an inflammatory condition, the member must have tried
and failed, or have a documented contraindication to, at least one conventional systemic therapy
for a minimum of 12 weeks. Step therapy exceptions are reviewed when the prescriber documents that
the required drug is expected to be ineffective or harmful.

## Review Timeframes

Standard specialty drug requests receive a decision within 72 hours of a complete request.
Expedited requests receive a decision within 24 hours.

## Initial and Renewal Authorization

Initial approvals are granted for 6 months. Renewal requests must include documentation of
clinical response, such as improvement in a validated disease activity score. Renewals are
granted for 12 months.

## Site of Care

Clinician-administered infusions are directed to a home infusion provider or an ambulatory infusion
center when clinically appropriate. Hospital outpatient infusion requires documentation of a
medical reason, such as a prior severe infusion reaction.

## Specialty Pharmacy

Approved self-administered specialty medications must be dispensed by the plan's designated
specialty pharmacy. The specialty pharmacy contacts the member within 2 business days of approval
to arrange delivery and provide injection training.
