# Sepsis Escalation FAQ for Nursing Staff

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional organization ("Maple Ridge Health System").
> Illustrative escalation workflow only. Not clinical guidance.

## When does the sepsis screen run?

The electronic sepsis screen runs automatically every 4 hours for adult inpatients, using vital
signs and laboratory values already documented in the record. Nurses may also start a manual
screen at any time if they are concerned.

## What happens when the screen is positive?

A positive screen triggers a best-practice alert. The bedside nurse must assess the patient and
notify the covering provider within 15 minutes of the alert. If the provider does not respond
within 15 minutes, the nurse escalates to the charge nurse and activates the rapid response team.

## What is the rapid response team?

The rapid response team is a critical care nurse and a respiratory therapist, with an intensivist
available by phone. The team can be called by any staff member, patient, or family member by
dialing extension 4-5555 from a hospital phone.

## What is included in the sepsis bundle?

Within 1 hour of sepsis recognition, the team obtains a lactate level and blood cultures, and the
provider decides on antibiotic therapy and fluid resuscitation. Repeat lactate is drawn within
6 hours if the initial value was elevated.

## How is the escalation documented?

The nurse documents the time of the alert, the time the provider was notified, and the actions
taken using the sepsis escalation note template.
