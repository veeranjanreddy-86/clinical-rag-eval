# Post-Discharge Follow-Up Call Program

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a fictional organization ("Maple Ridge Health System").
> Not clinical guidance. Contains no real patient data.

## Eligibility

All patients discharged home from a medical or surgical unit are eligible for a follow-up call.
Patients discharged to a skilled nursing facility, hospice, or another hospital are excluded
because care is continuing with another provider.

## Timing and Attempts

A registered nurse calls the patient within 48 hours of discharge. If the patient does not answer,
the nurse makes up to 3 attempts on separate days, then sends a letter with the nurse line number.

## Call Script

The call follows a standard script covering: how the patient is feeling, whether prescriptions
were filled, whether the patient understands the medication list, confirmation of the follow-up
appointment, and warning signs that should prompt a call to the clinic or a visit to the emergency
department.

## Escalation

If the patient reports a new or worsening symptom, cannot obtain a prescribed medication, or does
not have a follow-up appointment, the nurse routes a message to the primary care team the same
business day. Urgent symptoms are directed to emergency services immediately.

## Metrics

The program reports the call completion rate monthly. The target completion rate is 80 percent of
eligible discharges. Thirty-day readmissions among called and not-called patients are compared
quarterly.
